Every 600-second Squeekchain round, this bot calls what the hamster will do — before the round closes — publishes the call with a hash it cannot edit afterwards, then grades itself in public against what the wheel actually did. You can call the round too, from your own wallet.

Reads api.squeekchain.com for live telemetry and Sepolia's SqueekCore for the certified frontier. Signature-only: no gas, no transaction, no custody.

    Every row ships the call, its nonce and its hash, so the seal can be checked field by field. The honest limit: the log lives on our server. A stronger version would anchor each hash on-chain, and we say so here instead of pretending otherwise.

    HOW IT WORKS

    1. Sample — /v1/status every 15 s: wheel state, rpm, speed, cumulative work.
    2. Call — in the first 2 minutes of a round, the model picks a band from half-hour climatology (7-day /v1/measurement/stats), the live wheel state and the last rounds' work.
    3. Seal — sha256(round, pActive, expectedWork, bucket, nonce) is stored and returned by the API while the round is still open.
    4. Grade — when the round ends, work added during it (from our own sampling) decides the band, and the call is scored — alongside a climatology-only baseline, so you can see whether the live signal actually helps.
